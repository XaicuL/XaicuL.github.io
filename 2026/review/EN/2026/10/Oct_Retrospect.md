When the afterimage left by wishes that left my palms continues a quiet self-questioning

October gave me quite a lot of... um... accidents? You could call them accidents,
and thoughts? You could call them thoughts. That kind of month.

First, I sat the midterms not badly.
My days during midterms were staying up all night, taking the exam, sleeping, waking,
and studying again through dawn, days like that.

Statics was the same, and the other subjects too — not so much that I should blame myself
for studying carelessly, so it's an exam with leftover longing but little regret.

But October left me a gift? an insight? a way of thinking.
Honestly, my way of thinking until now was close to this:
if it doesn't violate nature or physical law,
that is, if it isn't a **theorem**, isn't anything possible?

Even when thinking about anything,
I would ask whether it was a **theorem** or a **definition**.
If it was a **theorem**, I thought I couldn't win unless I was that one time in ten,
and if it was a **definition**, I thought if I just set my mind to it I could win —
in the end, just humans nodding at each other,
nothing more than a convenience agreement?

This... broke.
Besides **definition** and **theorem**, an important variable was hidden.
But I can't even call it a variable. Maybe it's a constant.

I remember it was the day I was preparing for the last exam day.
We started exams on Tuesday and they ended the following Monday,
a truly strange exam structure.

The day preparing for the last day, so it was Sunday.
I already knew my great-aunt was unwell.

Even before that I had thought a lot about modern medicine,
and I think I had a lot of philosophical thoughts too.

Still I thought she had improved a lot,
so we promised to meet at my cousin's wedding on November 8,
and I ran even harder for midterms.

Back then I thought if I wanted to eat a lot, I'd use a lot of brain energy, get very hungry,
and at my cousin's wedding eat a lot of good food with my great-aunt,
so even on the call before I started preparing for midterms
I told her I'd see her then.

On Sunday I was already at the school lab
when Mom and Dad said my great-aunt was critical and they were moving to that region.
Between 11 p.m. and midnight a call came from Mom,
and I heard she had passed.

Honestly at that moment there was no change in how I thought.
The one thought that flashed through my brain was "Did she go peacefully."
Then eating one cloud cookie and just looking at the night sky, I thought I should still go see her.
Looking at trains, they were all sold out, impossible to book.

At that moment I really blamed myself a lot, though I don't know why.
Why did I come to a place like this, feet tied not for some other reason but for this,
why did I make that kind of judgment then, why?
Why am I here doing this now?

Like that... I really remember blaming myself a lot.
Less important than that, but what mattered was that mood must not become attitude,
and that came to me as the best task for the moment.

So my classmates probably didn't know.
If they somehow knew... that's a failed operation. I need more practice.
Anyway. I joked more and talked more, pretending nothing was wrong as much as I could.

Afraid that leaving even a little blank would bring an aftertaste,
I joked anyway and talked even more,
trying to reduce time alone as much as possible.

And when morning came, as soon as the TA arrived
I went to ask about attendance,
and the moment I heard attendance wouldn't be recognized

I ate cloud cookies one after another and blamed myself a lot again.
I couldn't hate anyone.
And looking back, it isn't my fault.
But I was just so angry.

I can't blame anyone.
It isn't the TA's fault,
and I can't say it's the fault of the president who made a rule like this,
or the president at the time.

But then I really felt a lot.
**Is this a theorem?** I still can't answer that.
The moment I settle it as **no**, then why can't it change?
Is this an axiom? A definition? Questions like that keep coming,

so it isn't that I can't give an answer,
it's closer to not giving one.

So looking back at that time, I think it was really hard.
Midterms... it was the last day too, so they weren't even on my mind,
I lay down pretending to be sleepy with my eyes closed and sorted a lot of thoughts...

If I remember, then I wanted to be alone and wished everything in the world would disappear,
and also didn't want to be alone? That kind of feeling... Let me unpack it more.

Being alone is scary, but even my own body is already too much,
I don't see a reason to look after anyone, and I'm not asking to be looked after,
I don't know why I'm like this, and thinking about it just makes me angry,
and I can't even show that outside, I just simmer it inside like nuclear fission,

I can say it was exactly that.
I think a lot of my thinking changed then, and even if it isn't nature,
even if it isn't physical law, I felt very physically
that many things can act as variables.

And then after midterms ended,
I went to my room to sleep a bit, and I think the feelings rushed in then.

Lying in the room, thinking one thing then another,
really, several feelings rushed in together.

One thought that still remains and hasn't healed much is
how hard must it have been?

Thinking about it, if it were me I think it would have been really too hard.

My child is about to get married and my body is sick —
first I'd feel so sorry to my child, and also wonder if that's even something to be sorry for,
and in the moment my body hurts beyond what willpower can hold,
what could I say to my child then.

Should I send encouragement
or send an apology
or send love

Thoughts like that came,
and thinking of memories with my great-aunt from when I was little,
the feeling then was quite overwhelming.

When I was little, when I was in kindergarten,
I thought she was the strongest person after my maternal grandmother, even more than Mom,
and as time passed and passed again, somehow I was taller than my great-aunt,
and from some point the figure in my childhood memory both showed and didn't show,
and just thinking those things one by one, I resented myself too,,

It didn't feel real at all. I didn't want to believe it,
and I even thought, maybe I stayed up so much my mind was unsteady
and I heard things, or just made a memory that isn't there?
I wished that were the case.

We were clearly going to meet on November 8 and greet each other,
I clearly said so on the phone and we promised to eat a lot then,
we did... we clearly did, and I don't know. No answer comes out.

Actually a new dream appeared in this process.
Ah, of course it didn't appear in this process — while preparing for midterms,
or maybe even before? I don't know. A dream appeared from some point,
and I decided to prepare for it.

I don't know what that dream is.
I didn't ask whether that dream can come find me in reality too.
But I decided to try once.

Let's wrap up!
But before that I want to use this space once however I want.

**From music heard by the ear,
and from the thinking heart,
I will come see you.**

**I will miss you a lot.
If you miss me during the trip, come anytime
I don't know when, but if I join that trip someday
then let's eat a lot of good things too. It's okay if you forget
I'll look it up, run over, and hug you.**

**But by then I'll be an adult too, right.
Then please introduce me to the lions and tigers you met on the trip,
and the friends you met there too.
Thank you for the figs. I love you, always, and I hope you are happy always.**

From Hyunjun Jeon.

Goodbye. And rest well.
