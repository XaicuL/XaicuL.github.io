
Passing the 31 gunshots that targeted May's bullseye over to June.

The 31 days felt incredibly short.
One moment it was May 12th, then suddenly the 22nd... "Wait, what did I even do?" But looking at the calendar, it wasn't spent scratching my belly watching Shorts; I was actually quite busy.
If someone came to me and asked, "So, are you satisfied?" I would probably say...

"I'm satisfied, but there's nothing to brag about!"
There was no return value, and I was just running, so there isn't much to boast about.
Think of it like the difference between jogging (running) and a short sprint race.

Both are great, and both are sources of satisfaction and pride, but for some reason, winning a sprint makes you go "Whoa! I did so well; the starting block was on my side."
Jogging, on the other hand, is physically exhausting, yet makes me think, "What did I actually gain from this time?" ~~My mind is definitely acting up.~~

One afternoon in May, a thought crossed my mind.
"Is it only 2 months, or already 2 months?"
It's been about 3 months since the semester started. Subtracting weekends (2 days x 4 weeks = 8 days), that leaves 24 days if we subtract 3 times, or 16 days if 2 times.
So it translates to roughly 2 and a half months, or slightly over 2 months.

Thinking about this... did I live well for those 2.5 months?
Just running day by day, some days I fell, some days I crashed, and some days I finally rose into orbit. As all those different days accumulated, they shaped the me who is living today.

May, as I resolved, was a Non-Return function itself. It returned nothing.
Just Math... Science... Research... Research... Science... Math...
Living like this feels like *Modern Times* at times, and *Inception* at others.

Recently, while talking with a younger peer in my department, a new thought occurred to me.
He didn't ask me this directly, but the thought just popped into my head, and I wanted to share it.

**"In this vast world, or even vaster universe, what kind of existence are we? If the giant Space of the universe is 100, has our civilization and technology even reached 1? Or maybe 0.9?"**

Since this thought struck me, my emotions have shifted quite a bit.
Our day-to-day lives, each hour I live, and even right now—the moment action potentials surge past the threshold and drop back down—how precious each of these moments is.

In such a day, especially within the tight constraints of a 24-hour day, to look back before falling asleep and feel no regrets but only satisfaction—what a grateful thing that is. ~~My apologies to whoever is reading this. I think I've caught the eighth-grader syndrome.~~

Where does autonomous thinking originate?

Thousands of these thoughts keep popping up...
It must all be a repetition of threshold -> action potential surge -> baseline -> threshold -> surge... etc.
